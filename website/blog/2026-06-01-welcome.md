# Welcome to the Zerolang Community

Welcome to the Zerolang community website!

## What is Zerolang?

Zerolang is an experimental systems programming language from Vercel Labs, launched in May 2026. Its unique characteristic: **from day one, it treats AI Agents as the primary user**.

Traditional programming language error messages require humans to read and understand them. Zerolang's compiler outputs structured JSON diagnostics that agents can parse and act on directly — no human translation needed.

## Goals of This Site

- **Documentation**: Systematic translation and curation of Zerolang official docs
- **Community**: Building a hub for developers to share and learn
- **Ecosystem**: Collecting tools, libraries, and projects related to Zerolang
